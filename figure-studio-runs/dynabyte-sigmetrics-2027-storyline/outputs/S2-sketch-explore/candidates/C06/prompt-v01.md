# C06 — One bounded memory-bin metaphor

Create one 16:9 raster research-paper conceptual schematic. Reviewer takeaway: the three uses occupy opportunity cost inside the same bounded GPU expert-memory pool. This is a qualitative capacity metaphor, not a measured memory breakdown.

## Narrative and layout grammar

Place one large outlined container labeled `Expert-memory budget` across 65% of the canvas. Inside it, create three clearly separated conceptual regions labeled `Residency`, `Precision upgrade`, and `Prefetch staging`, each with a compact internal motif. Do not assign numeric widths or equal fractions; use irregular but neutral conceptual spacing. Place `Coupled operating point` outside the right boundary, reached by one bundled output connector. Attach `memory bound` to the outer container and `quality constraint` to the precision-to-outcome route. Keep a narrow caption-support margin blank.

## Non-visual graph contract

Never render internal IDs. Containment inside the shared budget is the primary visual relation for allocation; therefore do not also draw three redundant budget arrows. The three contracted consequence relations may merge into one labeled connector family toward the coupled point, with each short label placed near its source port. No other edges.

## Visible text and motifs

Node/container labels: `Expert-memory budget`, `Residency`, `Precision upgrade`, `Prefetch staging`, `Coupled operating point`. Port/edge labels: `fewer avoidable misses`, `potential quality benefit`, `timely arrival / overlap`. Tags: `memory bound`, `quality constraint`. `bytes` may label the outer capacity boundary once.

Motifs: a few resident expert tiles; one low/high representation pair; one incoming staged tile; small selector/merge outside. No bullet lists.

## Hard constraints

No stacked bar chart appearance, no percentages, numeric capacities, proportional claims, axes, ticks, curves, heatmaps, legends suggesting data, measured allocation, or optimum marker. The region widths are illustrative only. One shared container and one conceptual process; do not duplicate experts into full workflows. Consequences stay on connector ports. No internal IDs or decorative arrows.

Surface: clean formal paper schematic, simple pool boundary, restrained colors, crisp labels, large whitespace; not a dashboard.
