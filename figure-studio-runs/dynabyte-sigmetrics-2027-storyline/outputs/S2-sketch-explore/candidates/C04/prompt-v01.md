# C04 — Radial opportunity-cost map

Create one 16:9 raster research-paper conceptual schematic. Reviewer takeaway: residency, precision upgrade, and prefetch staging are three distinct uses orbiting one bounded budget and feeding one coupled choice. This is a conceptual relation map, not a quantitative simplex.

## Narrative and layout grammar

Use a hub-and-spoke composition. Put `Expert-memory budget` as the central bounded pool. Place `Residency` upper-left, `Precision upgrade` upper-right, and `Prefetch staging` bottom-center, equally legible but not suggesting equal byte shares. Use one three-branch `bytes` allocation family from the center. Place `Coupled operating point` in a narrow lower-right anchor connected by a bundled consequence route from the three modules. Put `memory bound` and `quality constraint` beside the coupled point. Reserve a clean outer ring; no axes, triangle scale, or coordinate grid.

## Non-visual graph contract

Do not render internal IDs N_BUDGET, N_RESIDENCY, N_PRECISION, N_PREFETCH, N_POINT, N_MEMORY_BOUND, N_QUALITY_BOUND or any E_/P_/G_ identifier. Allowed relations are only the shared budget allocation fork, the three labeled consequence connectors to the coupled point, and two feasibility gates.

## Visible text and motifs

Node labels: `Expert-memory budget`, `Residency`, `Precision upgrade`, `Prefetch staging`, `Coupled operating point`. Connector labels: `bytes`, `fewer avoidable misses`, `potential quality benefit`, `timely arrival / overlap`. Gate tags: `memory bound`, `quality constraint`.

Motifs: capacity ring/bar at center; resident expert stack; narrow-to-wide representation cue; incoming expert approaching deadline; compact selector/balance at outcome. Keep all motifs pictorial and short-labeled.

## Hard constraints

This radial map must not look like measured ternary data: no triangle coordinates, axes, tick marks, color gradients, contours, data points, numerical shares, percentages, heatmap, or optimum star. Do not imply equal allocation. One shared budget; one instance of each use; one bundled allocation family; no module-to-module cross-links; no decorative arrows; no internal IDs. Consequences are edge labels, not boxes.

Surface: crisp formal publication schematic, light scientific colors and ample negative space, not an infographic poster.
