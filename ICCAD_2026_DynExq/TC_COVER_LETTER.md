# Cover Letter — IEEE Transactions on Computers

Dear Editor-in-Chief,

Please consider “DynaExQ: Budget-Safe Dynamic Expert Precision for
Single-GPU MoE Inference” as a Regular Paper for IEEE Transactions on
Computers.

The manuscript presents a fully resident dynamic-precision runtime for
Mixture-of-Experts inference under a fixed device-memory envelope. Its main
technical contributions are a routing-driven allocation policy, immutable
versioned expert handles with lease- and event-protected reclamation, and
admission-controlled fixed-block resident and staging pools. The evaluation
studies FP16/INT4 and INT4/INT2 precision pairs on three MoE model families,
including Qwen3-Next-80B.

The work fits the journal's scope in runtime systems, accelerator memory
management, software--hardware interaction, and experimental performance
evaluation for emerging machine-learning workloads.

This manuscript was previously submitted to the IEEE/ACM International
Conference on Computer-Aided Design (ICCAD) and rejected. It was not
published, there is no other version, and no portion is currently under
review elsewhere. All authors have approved this submission.
