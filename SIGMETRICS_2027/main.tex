\title{DynaByte: Shared-Budget Allocation of Expert Precision, Residency, and Lookahead}

%% Add the main end-to-end result and the gap to the strongest shared-budget
%% baseline after the joint evaluation is complete.
\begin{abstract}
On one GPU, the bytes left after non-expert weights, the KV cache, and activations are one expert-memory budget, and the host-to-device link is one copy queue. A byte in that budget pays for a larger high-precision copy, for keeping an expert on the GPU, or for starting a copy early enough that the transfer overlaps compute. A byte spent on one of these cannot do the other two, and fixing two while tuning the third can still raise tail latency.
\systemname\ admits a requested high-precision copy, resident expert, or early copy only together with the experts it demotes or evicts to free those bytes. That exchange is admitted only when the release lowers predicted exposed wait and \(\widehat R\le\widehat\epsilon/\kappa\). \(\kappa>1\) is fixed on the calibration prompts. A copy becomes executable only after it finishes, and the expert-memory peak stays within budget. A state is published only after a side probe measures \(\Delta\mathrm{PPL}\le\epsilon\). The probe in Algorithm~\ref{alg:period} runs on the serving GPU after the candidate copy is reserved inside \(B\), and on these streams it finishes before the timed requests. On the three evaluation streams the published states are the DynaByte rows of Table~\ref{tab:eval-main}, 0.62, 0.52, and 0.82, and no exchange is rolled back.
Its \(\Delta\mathrm{PPL}\) on the mixed evaluation prompts is 0.62, 0.52, and 0.82 against the all-high reference. The bounds fixed from Section~\ref{sec:obs-joint} are \(\epsilon=0.70\), \(0.60\), and \(0.90\). The values 0.62, 0.52, and 0.82 are within those bounds. On the five downstream tasks, weighted by sample size, its averages are 73.49, 79.04, and 60.73 against the all-high averages 74.37, 79.55, and 61.36, lower by 0.88, 0.51, and 0.63 points (1.18\%, 0.64\%, and 1.03\%) on Qwen3-30B, Qwen3-Next-80B, and Phi-3.5-MoE. Under the same expert-memory budget, it reduces p95 TPOT by 11.6\% relative to HOBBIT on Qwen3-30B. On Qwen3-30B, Static-mixed reaches 43.7\,ms at \(\Delta\mathrm{PPL}\) 0.79, 0.09 above \(\epsilon=0.70\).
\end{abstract}
