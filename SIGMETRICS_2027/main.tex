\title{DynaByte: Shared-Budget Allocation of Expert Precision, Residency, and Lookahead}

%% Add the main end-to-end result and the gap to the strongest shared-budget
%% baseline after the joint evaluation is complete.
\begin{abstract}
On a single GPU, expert memory and host-to-device bandwidth are shared among fidelity, residency, and lookahead. A byte assigned to one use cannot retain another expert or stage a future one, and a policy that fixes two uses and tunes the third can still raise tail latency.
\systemname\ admits one exchange at a time. The exchange names the lease it requests and the leases released to fund it, and it is admitted only when that release lowers predicted exposed wait and \(\widehat R\le\widehat\epsilon\). A representation is published only after its copy completes, and the executor holds the expert-memory peak within budget. \(\widehat R\le\widehat\epsilon\) is the controller's admission estimate; it is not the measured constraint \(\Delta Q\le\epsilon\).
On the five downstream tasks, weighted by sample size, \systemname's score is 1.18\%, 0.64\%, and 1.03\% below the all-high reference on Qwen3-30B, Qwen3-Next-80B, and Phi-3.5-MoE. Under the same expert-memory budget, it reduces p95 TPOT by 11.6\% relative to HOBBIT on Qwen3-30B.
\end{abstract}
