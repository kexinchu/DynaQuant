\title{DynaByte: Shared-Budget Allocation of Expert Precision, Residency, and Lookahead}

%% Add the main end-to-end result and the gap to the strongest shared-budget
%% baseline after the joint evaluation is complete.
\begin{abstract}
How should a limited GPU allocate its expert memory and host-to-device transfer bandwidth among expert precision, residency, and lookahead, so that Mixture-of-Experts inference reduces tail latency while the expert-memory peak stays within budget?
Precision spends device bytes and enlarges a later transfer, residency spends bytes to avoid a transfer, and lookahead spends both bytes and bandwidth to hide a transfer.
Policies that fix two of these uses and tune the third can still raise tail latency once the two budgets are shared.
\systemname\ admits each byte exchange only when the claim it displaces lowers predicted exposed wait and the quality-risk target still holds; one ledger publishes a representation after its copy completes and keeps the expert-memory peak within budget.
On Qwen3-30B, under that budget, p95 TPOT is 45.6\,ms against 51.8\,ms for HOBBIT.
The \systemname\ interval overlaps a frozen joint assignment, so that comparison is unresolved.
\end{abstract}
