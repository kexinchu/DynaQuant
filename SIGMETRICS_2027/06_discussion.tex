\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\subsection{Applicability and Boundary Regimes}
\label{sec:falsify}

The latency comparison uses budgets where the low-precision pack fits and the high-precision pack does not, \(1\le\PhiRatio<\rho\).
Above \(\rho\), every expert fits at high precision.
At 25\,GB/s, \systemname\ and \textsc{Static-Joint} center at 45.0\,ms and 45.6\,ms when \(\PhiRatio=1.0\), at 45.8\,ms and 48.6\,ms when \(\PhiRatio=1.3\), and at 44.4\,ms and 44.8\,ms when \(\PhiRatio=1.6\).
Three measured settings bound this comparison.
At the Qwen3-30B arena, \(\PhiRatio=1.931\), the low-tier pack fits, so a resident low-tier set copies no expert. \systemname\ and \textsc{Static-Joint} both center at 43.8\,ms.
Figure~\ref{fig:eval-necessity}(c) holds \(\epsilon=0.70\) fixed and varies host-to-device bandwidth. The gap to \textsc{Static-Joint} is 2.8\,ms at the measured copy rate and 0.4\,ms at 40\,GB/s.
Below 1, the low-tier pack does not fit. Section~\ref{sec:eval-end-to-end} measures \(\PhiRatio=0.7\) and \(B=10.78\)\,GB, below the 15.40\,GB low-tier pack. Every policy has \(\Delta\mathrm{PPL}=0.88\), above \(\epsilon=0.70\).
Algorithm~\ref{alg:period} admits a promotion only when that promotion satisfies Eq.~\eqref{eq:fit} and brings \(\widehat R\) to \(\widehat\epsilon/\kappa\). On this budget every executed row stays at the all-low value, so the repair leaves the run above \(\epsilon=0.70\). The measured \(\Delta\mathrm{PPL}\) on this budget is 0.88, and no promotion fits in \(B=10.78\)\,GB. At this common \(\Delta\mathrm{PPL}\), \systemname's p95 bracket lies below HOBBIT, DyMoE, and \textsc{Static-Joint}, and it overlaps Static-mixed and Uniform-low.

\subsection{Guarantees and Estimation Limits}
\label{sec:quality-boundary}

The executor holds the expert-memory peak within \(B\) and does not change routing.
The controller admits a copy when \(\widehat R\le\widehat\epsilon/\kappa\).
A state is published only when the side probe measures \(\Delta\mathrm{PPL}\le\epsilon\).
Section~\ref{sec:impl} fixes \(\kappa\) at \(1.10\), \(1.08\), and \(1.06\) before the evaluation prompts are read.
Eq.~\eqref{eq:risk} sums \(w_iq_i\) over low-precision experts.
Interactions among experts, and a shift in routing, can make that sum differ from measured \(\Delta\mathrm{PPL}\).

On Qwen3-30B, every state in Figure~\ref{fig:eval-validation}(b) with \(\widehat R\le\widehat\epsilon/1.10\) has measured \(\Delta\mathrm{PPL}\le\epsilon\).
The five states at median \(1.04\epsilon\) and maximum \(1.12\epsilon\) lie to the right of \(1/1.10\) and are not admitted.
If the probe exceeds \(\epsilon\), Algorithm~\ref{alg:period} rolls back the last admitted exchange. The published handles stay until a later probe is within \(\epsilon\).
On the evaluation streams the probe accepts the published states, the DynaByte rows of Table~\ref{tab:eval-main}.
Figure~\ref{fig:eval-necessity}(b) records the Qwen3-30B share and p95 change after the slice moves to decode-heavy. It does not plot \(\Delta\mathrm{PPL}\).
A copy staged from the current forecast prices a reload only inside that forecast's layers, so reuse beyond those layers can still be missed.
Section~\ref{sec:eval-models} measures the wait bias, Table~\ref{tab:eval-decision} is the sign agreement of \(G\), and its rejection row is computed after the run and is not the admission rule. Table~\ref{tab:eval-main} is the request-level p95 of Eq.~\eqref{eq:objective}.

\subsection{System Scope and Deployment Assumptions}
\label{sec:non-goals}

The current runtime manages one accelerator and uses host memory as its backing store.
Host memory retains both packed representations for the process lifetime; a deployment that cannot do so would need to quantize on demand or read a representation from storage, adding a transition not modeled here.
Resident-first execution assumes that expert groups within a layer can be issued independently.
A kernel that issues experts in a fixed order needs a different latency estimate.
Expert-parallel execution and an SSD tier are outside this design.
They add per-device capacity, a network or storage queue, and load imbalance, so the replay and the byte account have to include those queues.
Section~\ref{sec:eval-serving} includes serialization, loopback transport, tokenization, and queue wait on this host. A second GPU, a NIC between machines, and an SSD remain outside this runtime.

\subsection{Validity, Reproducibility, and Ethics}
\label{sec:threats}
\label{sec:ethics}

Sections~\ref{sec:obs-quality}--\ref{sec:obs-joint} motivate the constraints: quality sensitivity, routing variation, and transfer pressure.
Their absolute values are not results of the joint runtime; Section~\ref{sec:eval} is.
The measured streams are the five-task mix and the length-slice change in Figure~\ref{fig:eval-necessity}(b). The slices do not cover long contexts or multimodal requests, either of which can change routing breadth, the KV reserve, and available overlap.
Table~\ref{tab:eval-accuracy} weights tasks by item count, so AIME25 is 30 of 792 items.
One item moves MMLU-Pro, GPQA, AIME25, GSM8K, and HumanEval by 0.50, 0.51, 3.33, 0.50, and 0.61 points, and moves the average by 0.13 points.
For a column, the normal half-width \(1.96\sqrt{\hat p(1-\hat p)/n}\) is at most 6.9, 7.0, 17.5, 4.8, and 7.0 points on MMLU-Pro, GPQA, AIME25, GSM8K, and HumanEval. The normal approximation treats items as independent Bernoulli trials.
The policy takes measured bandwidth and kernel time as inputs.
Kernels, transfer paths, and quantization support can differ across devices.

A result is reproducible under Eq.~\eqref{eq:objective} only when the logs suffice to verify \(B\), \(\epsilon\), \(\kappa\), and the executed state transitions.
The study uses public latency traces and quality benchmarks, recruits no participants, collects no new user data, and sends no prompts to a third-party service.
Released artifacts will include the HOBBIT and DyMoE runners used for Table~\ref{tab:eval-main}, under the assumptions in Section~\ref{sec:eval-setup}, and will identify source prompts without redistributing material whose license forbids it.
