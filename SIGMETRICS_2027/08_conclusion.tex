\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

On one GPU, under the same expert-memory budget, \systemname\ reduces p95 TPOT by 11.6\%, 23\%, and 27.1\% relative to HOBBIT on Qwen3-30B, Qwen3-Next-80B, and Phi-3.5-MoE.
The lowest p95 centers in Table~\ref{tab:eval-main} are 43.7\,ms, 117\,ms, and 51.8\,ms. The rows at those centers have \(\Delta\mathrm{PPL}\) above \(\epsilon=0.70\), \(0.60\), and \(0.90\).
Its \(\Delta\mathrm{PPL}\) on the mixed evaluation prompts is 0.62, 0.52, and 0.82. These lie within \(\epsilon=0.70\), \(0.60\), and \(0.90\).
Weighted by item count, its averages are 73.49, 79.04, and 60.73 against 74.37, 79.55, and 61.36, lower by 0.88, 0.51, and 0.63 points (1.18\%, 0.64\%, and 1.03\%).

At equal or better \(\Delta\mathrm{PPL}\), the gaps to \textsc{Static-Joint} are 2.8\,ms and 27\,ms on Qwen3-30B and Qwen3-Next-80B.
On Qwen3-30B the gap is 0.4\,ms at 40\,GB/s, and at \(\PhiRatio=1.931\) both centers are 43.8\,ms.
On Phi-3.5-MoE the 5.2\,ms difference is 53.8\,ms against 59.0\,ms, at \(\Delta\mathrm{PPL}\) 0.82 against 0.76. Both values are within \(\epsilon=0.90\), and \systemname\ does not dominate \textsc{Static-Joint}. Uniform-low is at \(\Delta\mathrm{PPL}\) 0.97 and 51.8\,ms, above \(\epsilon=0.90\).
Demoting or evicting experts inside the one shared budget accounts for the 2.8\,ms and 27\,ms gaps.
Scoring the requested copy and the released expert separately leaves the center 0.3\,ms and 3\,ms lower, inside \systemname's interval.
At \(\PhiRatio=0.7\), every policy has \(\Delta\mathrm{PPL}=0.88\), above \(\epsilon=0.70\).
Under Poisson arrivals from a client on the same host, SLO goodput is 0.82 requests/s against HOBBIT's 0.69 on Qwen3-30B.
The runtime is one GPU with host memory as the only backing store.
