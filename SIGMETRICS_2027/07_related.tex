\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

Prior systems choose a precision, choose which experts stay on the GPU, or choose when a copy starts, often while holding the other two fixed.
We group them by that choice, then compare \systemname\ directly with systems that already combine the mechanisms.

\paragraph{Which precision is stored.}
LLM.int8()~\cite{dettmers2022gptint8}, GPTQ~\cite{frantar2022gptq}, SqueezeLLM~\cite{kim2024squeezellm}, AWQ~\cite{lin2024awq}, OmniQuant~\cite{shao2024omniquant}, and SmoothQuant~\cite{xiao2023smoothquant} fix representations before serving.
AQLM~\cite{egiazarian2024aqlm} and ZeroQuant~\cite{yao2022zeroquant} are further offline compressors for transformer weights.
QMoE compresses MoE weights below one bit per parameter~\cite{frantar2023qmoepracticalsub1bitcompression}.
MoE-specific methods use expert sensitivity, activation frequency, or router statistics to assign precision under a memory or quality target~\cite{kim2023mixturequantizedexpertsmoqe,chitty2025mopeq,chowdhury2026efficient}.
Such methods improve quality for a chosen footprint but commonly evaluate the assignment while which experts stay on the GPU and when copies start stay fixed.
\systemname\ uses their packed representations and offline sensitivities, and decides at runtime whether a larger high-precision copy is worth the resident experts or early copies it displaces.

\paragraph{Which experts stay on the GPU.}
SwapMoE~\cite{kong2024swapmoe}, MoE-Infinity~\cite{xue2024moe}, EdgeMoE~\cite{yi2023edgemoe}, and AdapMoE~\cite{zhong2024adapmoe} decide which experts stay and which are evicted from routing history, layer position, or co-activation, and the expert's precision stays fixed.

MoE-Lightning~\cite{cao2024moe}, SiDA~\cite{du2024sida}, and Fiddler~\cite{kamahori2025fiddler} compute experts that are absent from GPU memory on the CPU when GPU memory is short.
PowerInfer keeps frequently activated neurons of sparse feed-forward layers on the GPU and evaluates the remaining neurons on the CPU~\cite{song2024powerinfer}.

Yao et al.\ place co-activated experts by inter-layer affinity; that communication objective is outside this single-accelerator implementation~\cite{yao2024exploiting}.
DeepSeekMoE specializes experts in the model, and it is not a placement policy~\cite{dai2024deepseekmoe}.
Tutel~\cite{hwang2023tutel}, DeepSpeed-MoE~\cite{rajbhandari2022deepspeed}, and MegaBlocks~\cite{gale2023megablocks} run expert-parallel training and inference across devices.
In \systemname, keeping an expert on the GPU is scored together with the high-precision copy or early copy that would take its bytes, so the cost of evicting it is part of the same decision.

\paragraph{When the copy starts.}
Cross-layer prefetchers predict future experts and overlap their copies with current computation, using either a fixed depth or a depth adapted from observed demand~\cite{song2024promoe,eliseev2023fast,hwang2024pre}.
Shen et al.\ schedule that movement for a given expert size~\cite{shen2025expertflow}, and He et al., under the same name, combine a predicted cache with token scheduling~\cite{he2024expertflow}.

HOBBIT combines dynamic mixed-precision loading, adaptive prefetching, and a multi-level cache; its low-precision miss path reduces transfer cost for experts judged less critical~\cite{tang2024hobbit}.
DyMoE combines online expert importance, depth-aware mixed precision, caching, and lookahead prefetching, and some configurations omit selected experts~\cite{huang2026dymoe}.
HOBBIT and DyMoE already quantize, cache, and prefetch in one system.
\systemname\ admits a new copy only together with the experts demoted or evicted to free its bytes, and that decision includes the released expert's later reload.

In that table a HOBBIT miss loads the low-precision copy, and DyMoE keeps every expert the router selected, placing it in one of the two packed tiers.

\paragraph{Serving and memory management.}
vLLM~\cite{kwon2023vllm} and SGLang~\cite{zheng2024sglang} manage KV-cache memory, and Orca~\cite{yu2022orca} schedules requests at iteration granularity.
Sarathi-Serve~\cite{agrawal2024sarathi} chunks long prefills so decode iterations continue.
DistServe~\cite{zhong2024distserve} and Splitwise~\cite{patel2024splitwise} place prefill and decode on separate resources.
ZeRO-Infinity offloads training state across GPU, CPU, and NVMe, and FlexGen schedules dense-model inference across GPU, CPU, and disk~\cite{rajbhandari2021zeroinfinity,sheng2023flexgen}.
\systemname\ allocates the expert-memory budget left after those systems reserve non-expert state.
