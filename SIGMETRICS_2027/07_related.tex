\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

Prior systems control expert precision, residency, or transfer timing, often while holding the other decisions fixed.
We group them by that control decision, then compare \systemname\ directly with systems that already combine the mechanisms.

\paragraph{Precision assignment.}
GPTQ, AWQ, SmoothQuant, and LLM.int8() fix representations before serving~\cite{frantar2022gptq,lin2024awq,xiao2023smoothquant,dettmers2022gptint8}.
MoE-specific methods use expert sensitivity, activation frequency, or router statistics to assign precision under a memory or quality target~\cite{kim2023mixturequantizedexpertsmoqe,duanmu2025mxmoe,zhang2025moqe,chitty2025mopeq,chowdhury2026efficient}.
Such methods improve fidelity for a chosen footprint but commonly evaluate the assignment under fixed placement and transfer policies.
\systemname\ can use their packed representations and offline sensitivities; its additional question is whether a fidelity increment is worth the residency or lookahead displaced at runtime.
Mixed-precision kernels such as MxMoE remain complementary~\cite{duanmu2025mxmoe}: they determine the compute cost of a selected representation, not which state should occupy expert memory.

\paragraph{Residency and placement.}
Expert caches retain recently or frequently used experts and select victims using routing history, layer position, or co-activation~\cite{xue2024moe,li2023adaptive,yi2023edgemoe,zhong2024adapmoe,kong2024swapmoe,cao2024moe}.
They primarily estimate residency value with a fixed expert representation.
Placement methods instead colocate co-activated experts or map them according to topology and load~\cite{yao2024exploiting,dai2024deepseekmoe}; their multi-device communication objective is outside our single-accelerator implementation.
In \systemname, a residency lease is valued together with the fidelity or lookahead request that would displace it, so eviction cost belongs to the complete exchange rather than to an independent cache policy.

\paragraph{Prefetch and mixed-precision offloading.}
Cross-layer prefetchers predict future experts and overlap transfers with current computation, using either fixed lookahead or adaptation from observed demand and system state~\cite{song2024promoe,eliseev2023fast,hwang2024pre}.
ExpertFlow coordinates expert scheduling and movement under offload~\cite{shen2025expertflow}; a separate work with the same name changes token assignment to improve activation efficiency~\cite{he2024expertflow}.
Their principal control variable is transfer timing for a given expert size and quality policy.

HOBBIT combines dynamic mixed-precision loading, adaptive prefetching, and a multi-level cache; its low-precision miss path reduces transfer cost for experts judged less critical~\cite{tang2024hobbit}.
DyMoE combines online expert importance, depth-aware mixed precision, caching, and lookahead prefetching, including configurations that retain low-bit experts or omit selected experts~\cite{huang2026dymoe}.
Their designs establish that mixed precision, caching, and prefetching can coexist, so component composition is not a novelty claim for \systemname.
The distinction is whether one exchange, under one quality-degradation limit, decides both which bytes occupy expert memory and which transfers occupy the migration queue.
\systemname\ preserves top-\(k\) semantics and represents all three uses as byte leases.
Each request names its donor leases, and one deadline replay compares the resulting states under the quality-risk and byte accounts.
Matched-policy evaluation must establish this distinction; terminology or device class cannot.

\paragraph{Serving and memory management.}
vLLM and SGLang manage request scheduling and KV-cache memory, while FlexGen schedules dense-model computation and transfers across a memory hierarchy~\cite{kwon2023vllm,zheng2024sglang,sheng2023flexgen}.
\systemname\ operates on the expert-memory budget left after non-expert state is reserved and is complementary to those serving functions.
Because a serving engine can outgrow its declared KV or activation reserve, we report the runtime ledger and process-level peak separately.
