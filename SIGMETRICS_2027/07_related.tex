\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

Prior systems control fidelity, residency, or transfer timing, often while holding the other decisions fixed.
We group them by that control decision, then compare \systemname\ directly with systems that already combine the mechanisms.

\paragraph{Precision assignment.}
GPTQ, AWQ, SmoothQuant, LLM.int8(), OmniQuant, and SqueezeLLM fix representations before serving~\cite{frantar2022gptq,lin2024awq,xiao2023smoothquant,dettmers2022gptint8,shao2024omniquant,kim2024squeezellm}.
ZeroQuant and AQLM are further offline compressors for transformer weights~\cite{yao2022zeroquant,egiazarian2024aqlm}.
QMoE compresses MoE weights below one bit per parameter~\cite{frantar2023qmoepracticalsub1bitcompression}.
MoE-specific methods use expert sensitivity, activation frequency, or router statistics to assign precision under a memory or quality target~\cite{kim2023mixturequantizedexpertsmoqe,duanmu2025mxmoe,zhang2025moqe,chitty2025mopeq,chowdhury2026efficient}.
Such methods improve fidelity for a chosen footprint but commonly evaluate the assignment under fixed placement and transfer policies.
\systemname\ can use their packed representations and offline sensitivities; its additional question is whether a fidelity increment is worth the residency or lookahead displaced at runtime.
Mixed-precision kernels such as MxMoE remain complementary~\cite{duanmu2025mxmoe}: they determine the compute cost of a selected representation, not which state should occupy expert memory.

\paragraph{Residency and placement.}
Expert caches retain recently or frequently used experts and select victims using routing history, layer position, or co-activation~\cite{xue2024moe,li2023adaptive,yi2023edgemoe,zhong2024adapmoe,kong2024swapmoe,cao2024moe}.
These caches primarily estimate residency value with a fixed expert representation.
Fiddler evaluates experts that are absent from GPU memory on the CPU, and SiDA builds a data-aware plan for serving large MoE models in limited accelerator memory~\cite{kamahori2025fiddler,du2024sida}.
PowerInfer keeps frequently activated neurons of sparse feed-forward layers on the GPU and evaluates the remaining neurons on the CPU~\cite{song2024powerinfer}.
Placement methods colocate co-activated experts or map them according to topology and load~\cite{yao2024exploiting,dai2024deepseekmoe}; their multi-device communication objective is outside our single-accelerator implementation.
DeepSpeed-MoE and Tutel run expert-parallel training and inference across devices~\cite{rajbhandari2022deepspeed,hwang2023tutel}.
MegaBlocks supplies grouped sparse kernels for MoE training~\cite{gale2023megablocks}.
In \systemname, a residency lease is valued together with the fidelity or lookahead lease that would displace it, so eviction cost belongs to the complete exchange.

\paragraph{Prefetch and mixed-precision offloading.}
Cross-layer prefetchers predict future experts and overlap transfers with current computation, using either fixed lookahead or adaptation from observed demand and system state~\cite{song2024promoe,eliseev2023fast,hwang2024pre}.
ExpertFlow coordinates expert scheduling and movement under offload~\cite{shen2025expertflow}; a separate work with the same name changes token assignment to improve activation efficiency~\cite{he2024expertflow}.
Their principal control variable is transfer timing for a given expert size and quality policy.

HOBBIT combines dynamic mixed-precision loading, adaptive prefetching, and a multi-level cache; its low-precision miss path reduces transfer cost for experts judged less critical~\cite{tang2024hobbit}.
DyMoE combines online expert importance, depth-aware mixed precision, caching, and lookahead prefetching, and some configurations omit selected experts~\cite{huang2026dymoe}.
These systems establish that the three mechanisms can coexist.
The distinction is whether one exchange decides both which bytes occupy expert memory and which transfers occupy the migration queue.
Measured quality is reported separately from \(\widehat\epsilon\).
Section~\ref{sec:eval-setup} compares against the complete HOBBIT system under the same budget, representations, kernels, and request stream; DyMoE is not included, because the controller does not implement its estimator, depth-dependent bit schedule, or next-layer predictor.

\paragraph{Serving and memory management.}
Orca, vLLM, and SGLang schedule requests and manage KV-cache memory~\cite{yu2022orca,kwon2023vllm,zheng2024sglang}.
Sarathi-Serve chunks long prefills so decode iterations continue, and DistServe and Splitwise place prefill and decode on separate resources~\cite{agrawal2024sarathi,zhong2024distserve,patel2024splitwise}.
ZeRO-Infinity offloads training state across GPU, CPU, and NVMe, and FlexGen schedules dense-model inference across GPU, CPU, and disk~\cite{rajbhandari2021zeroinfinity,sheng2023flexgen}.
\systemname\ operates on the expert-memory budget left after non-expert state is reserved and is complementary to those serving functions.
Because a serving engine can outgrow its declared KV or activation reserve, we report the byte account and process-level peak separately.
